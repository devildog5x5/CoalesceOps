Services

The places an operation comes apart.

Each service is a defined piece of the same practice. Most engagements use more than one, because a split in one place is usually visible in another.

  1. 01

    Operating picture

    One view of the work in flight: what is open, who has it, and which commitment is at risk. Leadership and the floor read the same picture.

  2. 02

    Ownership

    A name on every duty that can stall the week, including the handoffs that currently belong to no one. Decisions that sit between groups get an owner.

  3. 03

    Cadence

    The meetings, reviews, and escalations that run the week, reduced to the ones that change the work. A calendar full of updates is not a cadence.

  4. 04

    Systems

    The tools and records that must agree. We join what the operation already uses. A replacement is recommended only when the current record cannot carry the work.

  5. 05

    Sites

    The same standard at every location that does the work, with room for what is truly local. A second site does not get to invent a second operation.

  6. 06

    Transition

    After a merger, a leadership change, or a platform cutover, the operation needs one way of working before the old ways harden into two companies again.
