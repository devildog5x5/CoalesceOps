The practice

One account of how the operation runs.

Coalesce Ops is an on-site practice. We examine the work as it is done, then leave a written account of what is split and what must join.

What “coalesce” means here

It does not mean a new slogan, a reorganization chart, or a tool that replaces the ones you already run. It means the separate pieces of an operation agree on the work, the owner, and the week.

The review covers the people who move the work, the systems that record it, and the leadership that owns the decisions. A gap in any one of those shows up in the others.

You receive a direct account: what is already sound, what is split, and the sequence in which to join it. The first visit is scoped in writing before it is scheduled. Work that follows is agreed separately.

What remains

The engagement is finished when your team can run the cadence without us in the room. Procedures, owners, and the record of what held stay with you.
