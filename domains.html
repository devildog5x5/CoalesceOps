Three names

One practice. Three domains.

CoalesceOps.com, CoalesceOps.pro, and CoalesceOps.org serve this website. Use the name that fits the introduction. The work, the method, and the contact are the same.

.com

CoalesceOps.com

The front door for clients. Start here for the practice, the services, and a conversation about an operation that has split. This is the canonical address for the site.

.pro

CoalesceOps.pro

The same firm, addressed to operators, advisors, and people who refer the work. The pages do not change. The .pro name marks the practice as professional work, not a product catalog.

.org

CoalesceOps.org

The published method: map, name, join, prove, leave. Client specifics stay with the client. The .org name is the public standard of the practice. It is not a separate organization.

How to point them

Each domain’s DNS should resolve to this same Hostinger site. Upload the site once into public_html. Add coalesceops.pro and coalesceops.org as domains on that hosting account, or redirect them to https://coalesceops.com/.

Mail and telephone do not change with the domain. Write to rmf@coalesceops.com or call 801.319.1061.
