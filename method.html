Method

Map. Name. Join. Prove. Leave.

The method is public. What is specific to your operation stays with you. CoalesceOps.org is the name we use when we are talking about the method itself.

  1. 01

    Map

    Watch how a real piece of work moves from request to done. The map is the path people use, including the side paths the official process does not mention.

  2. 02

    Name

    Put one owner on each duty that can stop that path. If two people believe they own it, or nobody does, that is the finding.

  3. 03

    Join

    Close the seams that matter first: a handoff, two records that disagree, or a site that runs a different week. Sequence the rest. Do not boil the ocean.

  4. 04

    Prove

    Run the new cadence on a real week. Record what held, what slipped, and what changed. A diagram that was never used is not a result.

  5. 05

    Leave

    Hand over the owners, the cadence, and the record. The team should be able to run the next week without a consultant in the meeting.
