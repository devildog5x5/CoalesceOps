Where the work splits

An operation can look unified on an organization chart and still run as several. A handoff has no owner. Two systems disagree about the same job. A second brand, or a company just acquired, still runs its own access, its own tools, and its own week.

Leadership then hears a different account than the people doing the work. A control written for an audit can describe a different operation than the one on the floor. Coalesce Ops watches how the work actually moves, including security, the vendor stack, and spend, and writes what must join for the operation to run as one.
